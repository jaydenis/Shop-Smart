# **SHOP SMART: SCENARIO TEMPLATES**

Use these scenarios with the rules in [shop_smart_lumo.md](shop_smart_lumo.md).

## **SCENARIO 1: THE HAUNTED WAREHOUSE**

| Field | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Objective** | Find 5 clues within the warehouse |
| **Setup** | Medium terrain, 8 shelves, 2 loading docks |
| **Hostiles** | 6x Shambling Zombie, 2x Possessed Mannequin |
| **Win Condition** | Collect 5 clues and extract via loading dock |
| **Clue Unlocks** | **Extraction Mission** — each clue removes a locked exit marker |
| **Comedy Beats In Play** | Wrong Conclusion, Obvious Trap, Reveal Costume |
| **Board Gags** | Random banana peels on floor, flickering lights every round |

**Scenario Rules:** Place 8 searchable shelf tokens. A crew member within 1" may use an action to Search one token; roll d20 vs. DR12. On success, gain one Clue. On a natural 20, also find one Spare Monster Mask; a crew member wearing it is ignored by hostiles until they attack, interact with loot, or a hostile ends within 1" and passes a Presence test (DR12). Once per Scenario, a crew member may spend 1 Clue before opening a container to learn its contents; if it contains a Monster, they make one free ranged attack as it appears. A crew member may spend 1 Clue to make an accusation; on a 1–2 on d6, animate one scenery piece as a Shambling Zombie.

## **SCENARIO 2: THE CURSED SHOPPING MALL**

| Field | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Objective** | Escape alive with the MacGuffin |
| **Setup** | Large board, food court center, 3 store entrances |
| **Hostiles** | 8x Shambling Zombie, 2x Demon Clown, 1x Possessed Cash Register (Boss) |
| **Win Condition** | Reach extraction point at mall entrance |
| **Clue Unlocks** | At 4 clues, reveal MacGuffin location |
| **Comedy Beats In Play** | Snack Confession, Wrong Conclusion |
| **Board Gags** | Escalators run backward occasionally, mannequins rotate positions |

**Scenario Rules:** Place 6 searchable store tokens. A crew member within 1" may use an action to Search one token; roll d20 vs. DR12. On success, gain one Clue. At 4 Clues, reveal the MacGuffin at a store entrance chosen by the opponent or GM. Once per Downed Monster, a crew member may consume a Courage Snack while within 1" of it to reveal one Clue or a hidden trap location. A crew member may spend 1 Clue to make an accusation; on a 1–2 on d6, animate one scenery piece as a Shambling Zombie.

## **SCENARIO 3: VAMPIRE MANOR**

| Field | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Objective** | Survive until sunrise |
| **Setup** | Dark conditions, 3 light sources available |
| **Hostiles** | 2x Vampire Familiars, 1x Night Manor Stalker, 1x Linda |
| **Win Condition** | Eliminate all Vampires OR survive 10 rounds |
| **Clue Unlocks** | Spend 2 clues = Decode the Map (ignore Darkness 1 round); 5 clues reveals Linda's ritual chamber |
| **Comedy Beats In Play** | Reveal Costume, Snack Confession |
| **Comedy Beats** | Vampires complain about modern architecture |

**Scenario Rules:** Place 5 searchable room tokens. A crew member within 1" may use an action to Search one token; roll d20 vs. DR12. On success, gain one Clue. At 5 Clues, reveal Linda's ritual chamber. A crew member may spend 2 Clues to ignore Darkness for one round. On a natural 20 while Searching, also find one Spare Monster Mask; its wearer is ignored by hostiles until they attack, interact with loot, or a hostile ends within 1" and passes a Presence test (DR12). Once per Downed Monster, a crew member may consume a Courage Snack while within 1" of it to reveal one Clue or Linda's ritual chamber.

## **SCENARIO 4: FULL MOON NIGHT**

| Field | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Objective** | Neutralize wolf threat before morning |
| **Setup** | Outdoor forest terrain, moonlight provides darkness immunity |
| **Hostiles** | 2x Full-Moon Ravagers, 1x Manager Mike, Evil Twin, d4+1 Cursed Shopping Carts |
| **Win Condition** | Kill all hostile copies before morning |
| **Comedy Beats In Play** | Wrong Conclusion, Reveal Costume |
| **Comedy Beats** | The evil twin rolls in with a swarm of cursed shopping carts and mocks the crew with manager jargon |

**Scenario Rules:** Place 5 searchable forest tokens. A crew member within 1" may use an action to Search one token; roll d20 vs. DR12. On success, gain one Clue. At 5 Clues, reveal a cache containing enough Silver weapons for the crew. On a natural 20 while Searching, also find one Spare Monster Mask; its wearer is ignored by hostiles until they attack, interact with loot, or a hostile ends within 1" and passes a Presence test (DR12). A crew member may spend 1 Clue to make an accusation; on a 1–2 on d6, animate one scenery piece as a Shambling Zombie.

## **SCENARIO 5: FINAL CONFRONTATION**

| Field | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Objective** | Defeat the Hex Mall Warlock |
| **Setup** | Tower lair, 3 floors, magical barriers |
| **Hostiles** | 3x Demon Clown, 1x Hex Mall Warlock (Boss) |
| **Win Condition** | Reduce Boss HP to 0 |
| **Clue Unlocks** | At 5 clues, roll d20 + clues vs DR25 — success reveals Vriprix's true weakness (his Armor drops to 0 for the finale) |
| **Comedy Beats In Play** | All Comedy Beats available |
| **Comedy Beats** | Spell misfires cause silly effects (chicken transformation, singing) |

**Scenario Rules:** Place 5 searchable study tokens. A crew member within 1" may use an action to Search one token; roll d20 vs. DR12. On success, gain one Clue. At 5 Clues, roll d20 + Clues vs. DR25; on success, the Hex Mall Warlock's Armor becomes 0 for the rest of the Scenario. This Scenario may use any of the special rules from Scenarios 1–4 when their named Comedy Beat is selected.
