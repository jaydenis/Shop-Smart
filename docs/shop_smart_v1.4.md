## SECTION 1: CLUE PROGRESSION

Clues are scenario tokens representing evidence the crew collects. A Scenario states where its clues are found, how they are collected, and any special effects that use them. Clues are not spent unless that Scenario explicitly says so.

| Step | Mechanic |
| --- | --- |
| **Collect** | Crew gathers clues using the Scenario's clue rules |
| **Threshold** | Scenarios list a Clue Threshold (default **5**) |
| **Unlock** | Once the threshold is reached, the Scenario's win condition becomes available |

**Evidence Retention Rule:** Clues that have been spent (accusations, trap checks, decoding, etc.) still count toward any Clue Threshold or finale roll. Evidence doesn't disappear just because you used it.

## SECTION 7: INJURY & TRAUMA TABLE

When a crew member is reduced to 0 HP, they are Downed. If they fail their death save at the end of the scenario (or if a rule forces a permanent injury check), do not roll for tragic, grimdark mutilation. Instead, roll a d20 on the table below to see what kind of cartoony, embarrassing, or physically absurd trauma they have sustained.

| d20 Roll | Injury Name | Permanent Flaw / Penalty | Slapstick Narrative Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1–2 | Full Body Cast | -2 MV for the next scenario. | You were flattened like an accordion by a falling shelf. You spend the next mission entirely wrapped in white gauze, squeaking like a dog toy when hit. |
| 3–4 | Squeaky Shoes | Cannot enter Stealth. Hostiles can always see you in the dark. | You stepped directly into a bucket of industrial adhesive and high-gloss floor finish. Every step you take produces an incredibly loud, high-pitched *SQUEAK*. |

| 9–10 | The Permanent Scaredy-Cat | Must pass a PRE DR12 test or flee 3" whenever a door opens. | Your hair is permanently standing on end, and your eyes are wide with shock. A sudden *"BOO!"* might send you leaping straight into a teammate's arms. |
